Village-style Fried Rice

A no-fuss dish that’s sure to be a favorite!

Ingredients

  • 6 scoops of cooked white rice
  • 30 g anchovies (briefly fried)
  • 1 egg
  • A pinch of Himalayan salt
  • 1 tsp MSG-free stock seasoning
  • 2 tbsp sweet soy sauce
  • 2 stalks of celery (sliced)
  • 5 red chilies
  • 3 shallots
  • 1/4 onion
  • 1 clove of garlic
  • 1 packet of ABC shrimp paste
  • 4 lettuce leaves
  • 4 slices of tomato
  • 4 slices of cucumber
  • Enough omelet, sliced into strips
  1. Sauté the blended spices until fragrant, then add the egg and scramble until fully cooked.
  2. Add the rice once it is no longer clumped together, and stir until evenly combined.
  3. Add the anchovies, Himalayan salt, sweet soy sauce, and stock seasoning. Stir well, then add the celery and mix again. Taste and adjust the seasoning.
  4. Transfer the fried rice to a plate and garnish.
  5. Ready to serve.
